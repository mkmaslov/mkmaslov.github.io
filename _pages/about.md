I am a theoretical physicist seeking to transition into quantitative finance.

**My preference:** roles focused on investment or trading *strategy research*, portfolio or risk modelling. I am specifically interested in roles that require designing and evaluating decision-making algorithms, where coding is a research tool rather than the end product.

**My background:**
- *PhD in Theoretical Physics* from [Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA)](https://ista.ac.at)
- *BSc in Applied Mathematics and Physics* from [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT)](https://mipt.ru/english/)
- 7 publications (3 as the first-author, +2 more under review), 170 citations
- ~10 years of physics research experience, including major conferences and successful grant applications

**Selected research achievements:**
- Designed and built from scratch a neural-network solver for a class of quantum few-body differential equations, combining reinforcement learning and Monte Carlo sampling (under review)
- Developed a general analytical framework that describes angular momentum transfer between light and matter (published in [Physical Review Research](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.033277))

**Additional expertise:** scientific Python, numerical optimization, variational methods, Fourier analysis, machine learning, tensor calculus, group theory.
