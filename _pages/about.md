I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Institute of Science and Technology Austria](https://ist.ac.at/home).

**I am seeking a role in quantitative finance** focused on investment and trading strategy research, portfolio or risk modelling. My focus is the design and evaluation of decision-making algorithms across asset classes, especially where coding is a research tool rather than the end product. I am especially interested in systematic strategies, multi-asset investing, and the strategy side of high-frequency trading.

**My work philosophy:**
<details>
    <summary><strong>scientific method</strong>: understanding drives efficiency</summary>
    <div class="details-body">
        <p>
        <em>Efficient systems improve through an observation–analysis-iteration loop. Intuition can start the process, but it does not scale. Only feedback-driven refinement survives repeated trials.</em>
        </p>
    </div>
</details>
<details>
    <summary><strong>clear communication</strong>: clarity is the proof of mastery</summary>
    <div class="details-body">
        <p>

        </p>
    </div>
</details>
<details>
    <summary><strong>simplicity</strong>: the simplest sufficient explanation is the best</summary>
    <div class="details-body">

<details>

    <div class="details-body">

        </p>
    </div>
</details>
