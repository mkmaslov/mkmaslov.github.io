2 publications (+2 more under review); reviewer for *Phys. Rev. A* and *Phys. Rev. Research*

* Built from the ground up a neural-network solver for quantum few-body problems:
    * Implemented Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling and designed network representation that embeds rotational symmetry as a universal inductive bias.
    * Trained the network using system energy as a single objective, avoiding relying on manually tuned or arbitrarily defined hyperparameters.
    * Validated convergence against a DVR benchmark across a broad range of system scales.
* Developed a symmetry-aware mathematical model for optical sensing of material chirality.
